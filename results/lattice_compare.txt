Four discs in a square lattice against the single moving-PML sweep and the
bi-directional alternation of Zepeda-Núñez & Zhao (SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 38 (2016)
B866-B888, Alg. 2: sweep along x1, form the residual, sweep it along x2, add), on
the sparsified Lippmann-Schwinger system of Liu & Ying (examples/lattice_compare.jl).

Discs of radius R = 0.15 centred at (1/2 +- 0.2, 1/2 +- 0.2) in the unit square,
m = m_in inside with a sharp edge (m_in = -1 is n^2 = 2, c_in = 1/sqrt 2; m_in = -3 is
n^2 = 4), 0 outside.  h = lambda/ppw in the background, lambda/(ppw/sqrt(1-m_in)) inside
the discs; b = 8, C = 12, GMRES(60) to 1e-6 (flexible GMRES for the two-level rows).
One process with 8 threads (4 or 6 for the variant runs) on a 16-core Ryzen 7950X3D;
the sweep itself is serial, so the thread count only touches the setup.

  julia --project=. -t 8 examples/lattice_compare.jl 8 16 32 64 128
  julia --project=. -t 4 examples/lattice_compare.jl 32 64 --dir=1,1 --only=direct,x1,x2,alt,bidir,x1i6,bidiri6
  julia --project=. -t 4 examples/lattice_compare.jl 32 64 --dir=1,0 --only=direct,x1,x2,bidir
  julia --project=. -t 4 examples/lattice_compare.jl 32 64 --m=-3 --only=direct,x1,x2,alt,bidir,x1i6,bidiri6
  julia --project=. -t 6 examples/lattice_compare.jl 16 32 --m=-3 --ppw=16 --only=direct,x1,x2,alt,bidir,x1i6,bidiri6
  julia --project=. -t 6 examples/lattice_compare.jl 32 --ppw=16 --only=direct,x1,x2,bidir,x1i6,bidiri6

COLUMNS.  setup: sparsification, H and the factorisation(s) -- two of them for an
alternating row; apply: one preconditioner application, timed once; outer: iterations
of the Krylov solver on the dense system, "!" if it hit the cap of 300; sweeps: slab
sweeps performed inside that solve (an alternating application performs one per axis;
LU solves on the exact-H row); fwd: dense forward maps; solve: the Krylov solve,
setup excluded; relres: true relative residual of the dense system; vs direct:
distance of the row's solution from the exact-H row's, relative; peak: resident set.

ROWS.  "x1 sweep, two fronts" is the package default; "x2" the same slices cut along
x2; "one front" the plain left-to-right sweep; "x1 then -x1" two one-front sweeps in
opposite directions on the same axis, the second applied to the residual of the first;
"bi-directional x1,x2" Alg. 2 of Zepeda-Núñez & Zhao; "x1,x2,x1" a third pass; "inner k"
GMRES on H to 1e-3, at most k iterations, inside every application (their Alg. 3
when combined with the bi-directional row).

NOTE on the m_in = -3 runs at ppw = 8: these have 4 points per wavelength inside the
discs and are kept only to show what under-resolution does to the sweeps; the
ppw = 16 runs (8 inside) are the ones to read.  The headers of the raw output below
were printed by an earlier version of the script that inverted the interior
resolution; they have been corrected by hand (5.7 and 4.0 inside at ppw = 8).
