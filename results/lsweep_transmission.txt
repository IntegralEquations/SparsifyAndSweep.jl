Scenario 1 of Taus et al. on the sparsified operator H: 2x2 CDD of 64-point boxes
with the corner at the centre of the medium, source in box Omega11, the three
stages of an L-sweep -- local solve, two straight transmissions through the
cardinal interfaces, one diagonal transmission through the L-shaped trace.
Relative error of each box against the global solution lu(H) \ f.

  julia --project=. -t 16 examples/lsweep_transmission.jl 32 64 128

Omega21 and Omega12 agree to all printed digits: the configuration is symmetric
about the diagonal.  The straight transmissions halve with each doubling of the
absorber and then flatten near the accuracy of the fitted stencils themselves;
the diagonal step through the L-shaped trace is as accurate as the source box.
